# Pool & Pest Service App --- Full Technical Specification

**Platforms**: Expo (React Native, JavaScript) mobile app + React
(JavaScript) web/host app\
**Audience**: Field technicians, dispatch/admins, customers (optional
portal)\
**Verticals**: Pool cleaning (primary), pest control (secondary) ---
designed as a reusable field‑service platform

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1) Goals & Scope

**Primary goals** - Replace paper/texting with one system for
**scheduling, routing, service logging, photos, inventory, and
invoicing**. - Enable **fast daily operations** (route planning,
rescheduling, proof of service) with **offline-first** field capture. -
Offer **customer visibility** (reports, invoices, payments) and **admin
reporting** (revenue, usage, inventory).
