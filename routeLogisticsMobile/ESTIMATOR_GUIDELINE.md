What to Include in a Handyman Estimate
1) Header & Identity

Business name, logo, license # (if applicable)

Address, phone, email, website

Insurance proof note (GL/Workers’ Comp; bonded if applicable)

Customer name, service address, billing address (if different)

Estimate number, issue date, valid-until date (e.g., 15–30 days)

Project/Job name (short description)

2) Scope of Work (Exact and Bounded)

Plain-English description of tasks (broken into bullet points)

Location(s) on site (e.g., “Master bath,” “South fence line”)

Deliverables/finish level (e.g., “paint ready,” “stain match existing”)

Measurements & quantities (linear ft, sq ft, count)

Materials responsibility: who supplies what

Disposal & haul-away included or not

Tip: Pair each task with a measurable unit and acceptance criteria (e.g., “Replace 20 lf baseboard, caulk and paint to color match within ±3% sheen.”)

3) Explicit Exclusions & Assumptions

Exclusions (e.g., “no structural, no roofing beyond patch,” “no tile beyond listed counts”)

Hidden conditions clause (rot, mold, asbestos, knob-and-tube, ungrounded outlets)

Access assumptions (water, power, parking, elevator/freight)

Client prep (clear furniture 6 ft from walls, cover valuables, crate pets)

HOA/permit rules (if any) and who handles them

4) Materials & Equipment

Materials list (brand/model/SKU where helpful)

Finish specs (paint brand/sheens, fastener type, caulk type)

Rental equipment (e.g., 28’ ladder, auger, pressure washer)

Lead times or special orders (restocking fees if canceled)

5) Labor & Pricing Breakdown

Pricing model: Fixed price or Time & Materials (T&M)

Labor rate(s): standard, helper/apprentice, specialty (electrical/ plumbing tasks)

Estimated hours per task (even for fixed price, show how you arrived there)

Minimum service charge (e.g., 1- or 2-hour minimum)

Travel/mobilization fee (if applicable)

After-hours/weekend/expedited rates (if applicable)

6) Taxes, Fees, & Overheads

Sales tax (if applicable) and how it’s applied

Disposal/haul-away fees

Permit/inspection fees

Fuel/trip charges or parking

Small consumables (tape, blades, rags) policy (line item or included)

7) Schedule & Access

Target start window and duration

Work hours (e.g., 8am–5pm)

Access instructions (lockbox code, on-site contact)

Weather dependencies (for exterior work)

Delay clauses (materials backorder, discovery of hidden damage)

8) Quality, Warranty, & Code

Workmanship warranty (e.g., 12 months on labor; manufacturer warranty on parts)

Limits of warranty (wear-and-tear, owner-supplied materials)

Code compliance statement (to the extent applicable for handyman scope)

9) Change Orders

Process for out-of-scope discoveries

How changes are priced (rate sheet or fixed CO)

Written approval requirement before proceeding

10) Payment Terms

Deposit (e.g., 20–50% on acceptance; note state rules)

Progress payments (milestones) or T&M billing cadence (weekly)

Final payment due on substantial completion/inspection

Accepted payment methods (ACH, card, check; card fee policy)

Late fees, returned check policy

Right to stop work for non-payment

Mechanic’s lien notice (jurisdiction dependent)

11) Safety & Site Protection

Dust control, coverings, HEPA vac where needed

Pet and child safety responsibilities

Lead paint/RRP (for pre-1978 homes, if applicable)

Pool/electrical lockout/tagout precautions (if applicable)

12) Signatures & Authorization

Customer signature, printed name, date

Contractor signature, printed name, date

Checkbox/initials acknowledging exclusions & change-order policy

13) Attachments (Recommended)

Photos of existing conditions

Marked-up sketches or simple diagrams with dimensions

Manufacturer cut sheets/specs for special materials

Proof of insurance/licensing (PDF)

Rate sheet (if T&M)

Optional Sections by Service Type

Pest Control

Treatment plan (targets: ants/roaches/rodents; interior/exterior zones)

Chemical names/EPA reg #; safety data sheet availability

Follow-up/retreat policy; recurring schedule options (monthly/quarterly)

Pool & Spa

Water testing panel and targets (pH, TA, CH, CYA, FC)

Equipment serviced (pump/filter/heater models)

Weekly vs. bi-weekly plan; storm cleanup policy

HVAC/Appliance

Model/serial numbers

Filter sizes & replacement cadence

Thermostat compatibility notes

Painting

Surface prep steps (clean, sand, patch, prime)

Masking and protection list (floors, fixtures)

Coat count and sheens by room/surface

Red Flags to Avoid

Vague scope (e.g., “Fix fence” without footage/hardware)

No exclusions (“Everything included”)—invites disputes

No valid-until date

No change-order process

No warranty clarity (owner-supplied materials are common exceptions)

Cash-only with no invoice/receipts
